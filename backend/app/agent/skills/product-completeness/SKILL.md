# Product completeness review

Inputs: approved brief, shared decisions, nine documents, relevant supplied source.
Check the target service's journeys, roles and action permissions; create/edit/
delete/archive behavior; lifecycle transitions; error and empty states; versioning
and retention; search boundaries; integrations; MVP/non-goals; measurable outcomes.
Ask only for missing decisions that matter for this product. Differentiate facts,
user-confirmed choices, proposals, assumptions and externally verified evidence.
For each finding, cite the exact document line and quote, describe the consequence,
classify specification/implementation/production/future impact, and identify
affected documents and a concrete next action. Do not substitute your preferred
product defaults for a decision. Mark irrelevant checks as not applicable with a
reason. A simple tool does not need a marketplace's complexity.
